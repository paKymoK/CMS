01

The challenge

Traders priced intraday positions from a patchwork of spreadsheets and weather feeds. Each forecast took up to three hours to refresh, and by the time it did, the market had often moved.

The client needed forecasts that updated continuously, explained their reasoning, and fit into an existing trading desk without retraining the team.

02

Our solution

A CMC Global squad of nine built a streaming forecasting pipeline on Azure, combining gradient-boosted models for demand with a transformer model for price movement.

  • Feature store that ingests market, weather and grid data every 60 seconds.
  • Explainability panel so traders see the drivers behind each price signal.
  • MLOps with automated back-testing before any model is promoted.
03

The outcome

Within two quarters of launch the desk was trading on fresher signals with fewer manual overrides, which fed directly into revenue.
