# About CMC Global

CMC Global is the international IT services arm of CMC Corporation, one of
Vietnam's leading technology groups, founded in 1993 and headquartered at
CMC Tower, 11 Duy Tan Street, Cau Giay, Hanoi, Vietnam. CMC Global focuses
specifically on serving clients outside Vietnam — global enterprises looking
for an offshore/nearshore delivery partner for software engineering, cloud,
data, and AI work.

## What CMC Global does

CMC Global's services fall into three broad groups:

**World-class IT Outsourcing**
- Custom Software Development
- Software Maintenance
- Legacy Migration
- Testing Services

**Digital Transformation**
- Cloud Professional Services
- Data & Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence Solutions
- RPA (Robotic Process Automation) Services
- Low Code development

**CMC Solutions** — in-house products built and supported by CMC Global:
- IoT Smart Device (CIVAMs Face)
- CMC Social Listening
- CMC Chatbot
- C-ID Reader
- C-CA
- SOC (Security Operations Center)

## How clients engage CMC Global

CMC Global supports several engagement models, so clients can pick whichever
fits their project rather than being forced into one shape:

- **Project-based** — CMC Global owns delivery of a defined scope end to end.
- **Staff Augmentation** — CMC Global engineers embed directly into a
  client's own team.
- **Hybrid** — a mix of dedicated delivery and embedded staff.
- **Results Based** — engagement tied to measurable outcomes rather than
  time or headcount.

## Global footprint

CMC Global delivers from a network of offices spanning Vietnam (Hanoi
headquarters and Ho Chi Minh City), Japan (Osaka), Korea (Seoul), Singapore,
Australia (Sydney), the United States (San Jose), and Germany (Frankfurt) —
giving clients delivery coverage across APAC, ANZ, DACH, and North America
time zones.

## Notable clients

CMC Global has delivered projects for global brands including Bosch, Honda,
AIA, IBM, and LINE, spanning industries such as automotive, insurance,
